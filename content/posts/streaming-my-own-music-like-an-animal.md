Amazon Music, Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music. Across the services I tried,
there was always something: missing content, music unavailable in my country,
or playback trouble. Amazon Music's interface was absolutely horrid; I found it
unusable. Apple Music would stutter on my Android phones often enough to become
deeply annoying.

Eventually, I quit streaming services for my primary listening. Paying for
convenience had become an oddly demanding hobby.

I installed **gonic on my Mac Mini M4**, specifically for its folder view.
Apparently, wanting to browse music in the folders I put it in is now a
specialised requirement.

Even finding a client was frustrating. The often recommended
[Symfonium](https://symfonium.app/) had overlapping interface elements on my
new Oppo Reno 16 Android phone, making it barely usable.

We even opened a [pull request](https://github.com/yuzicapp/yuzic-engine/pull/15)
to add ALAC playback support to Yuzic on Android. I wanted to listen to music;
now I'm contributing to a playback engine.

Have I jumped into another deep rabbit hole? I hope not!

So I'm streaming my own files locally again. *Like an animal.* Maintaining a
collection, running a server, taking responsibility for playback. The chores
my subscriptions were supposed to eliminate.

There's something quietly absurd about all this: after trying services offering
millions of songs, I'm back to keeping the ones I actually want to hear.

Projects worth checking out:

- [gonic](https://github.com/sentriz/gonic)
- [SubTUI](https://github.com/MattiaPun/SubTUI)
- [Yuzic](https://github.com/yuzicapp/yuzic)
